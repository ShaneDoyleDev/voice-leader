Grand Staff Sketchpad

Move over either staff and a faded note snaps to the nearest line or space. Click to place it, drag it to another pitch, or click it again to take it off. Ledger lines appear on whichever staff keeps them fewest.

On the staff

    Keyboard: Tab to the staff, ← → for position, ↑ ↓ for pitch, Enter to place or remove.
